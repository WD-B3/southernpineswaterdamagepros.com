Water Damage Restoration in Downtown Southern Pines, NC

Water removal and drying for the shops, offices and homes around Broad Street.

☎ Call (910) 387-8176 · 24/7

Downtown Southern Pines is a working center, not just a postcard. The Historic District nomination describes a plateau of commercial blocks along Broad Street on both sides of the Seaboard railway, with houses spreading out on a square grid. Commercial blocks and older houses sit within a few blocks of each other, so water damage here can involve a business, a home or both.

What the record tells us about the buildings

The nomination lists downtown landmarks such as the Belvedere Hotel Building from 1904, the Arcade Building from 1917, the Seaboard Air Line passenger depot, the 1937 post office and the 1939 public library. North of the tracks, it describes late Victorian and Queen Anne cottages around Bennett Street, the original main avenue. South of Broad Street it describes many shingled cottages and Craftsman bungalows.

Older commercial buildings often share walls, upper floors and plumbing runs, so water can travel between units. That is a characteristic of the building type, not a claim about what happens in any one block. We did not verify FEMA flood-zone status for the neighborhood, and a map lookup for the individual property is the right check.

Verified storm context

The Town of Southern Pines reported flash flooding on July 6, 2025, during Tropical Storm Chantal, with estimated rainfall of 7 to 8 inches in its first release. Its list of road impacts included sinkholes on East Pennsylvania Avenue between Broad Street and Ashe Street and on West Pennsylvania Avenue at South Mechanic Street. The release did not report building damage in downtown, so we make no claim about it.

The Town also reported that its sanitary sewer lift stations stayed operational, with no overflows in its first release. Later releases identified five sanitary sewer overflows, two of them tied to dam failures elsewhere in town. For drain or sewage problems in a building, see sewage backup cleanup.

Water damage scenarios we handle downtown

  • A supply line or sprinkler line leak in a shop that wets the unit below.
  • A rooftop drain or flat roof leak above an upstairs office.
  • An apartment water heater or washer failure that reaches the ceiling beneath.
  • Storm water coming through doors or windows during heavy rain.
  • A drain backup in an older building.

For businesses, see commercial water damage restoration, which covers keeping records for an insurer and planning drying around opening hours. For ceilings that have already stained, see ceiling water damage repair.

First steps for a business or resident

  1. Stop the source if you can do so safely, and tell your landlord or building manager.
  2. Keep people away from wet electrical equipment and standing water.
  3. Move inventory, files and electronics off wet floors.
  4. Photograph damage and note the time you found it.
  5. Call us, and call your insurer to ask what documentation it needs.

Residents near Weymouth Heights can see our Weymouth Heights page, and the West Southern Pines page covers the neighborhood to the west.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with commercial buildings downtown?

Yes. Tell us the type of building and whether other units are affected. We explain the scope and price before work starts.

Did downtown Southern Pines flood during Chantal?

The Town reported flash flooding and road sinkholes in town, including on Pennsylvania Avenue. We have no verified report of building damage downtown.

Can water travel between shops in an older building?

It can, through shared walls, floors and ceilings. Moisture readings in neighboring spaces show how far it has spread.

Who handles drying, the landlord or the tenant?

That depends on your lease and policy. Ask your landlord and insurer, and keep photos and records.

Is downtown in a FEMA flood zone?

The status of the downtown blocks was not verified here. Search the building address on the FEMA map service to find out.
