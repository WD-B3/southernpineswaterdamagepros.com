Carpet Water Damage Restoration in Southern Pines, NC

Carpet and pad hold a lot of water. What happens next depends on the water source and how fast drying starts.

☎ Call (910) 387-8176 · 24/7

Carpet water damage restoration in Southern Pines, NC deals with wet carpet, pad and the subfloor below. Carpet is two layers, the visible fibers and the cushion beneath, and the pad holds far more water than the surface suggests. Whether the carpet can be saved depends on the water source, how long it stayed wet and how fast drying begins.

Lift furniture off the carpet, put aluminum foil or plastic under legs to stop staining, and open windows only if the weather and security allow. Do not use a household vacuum on soaked carpet. Call so extraction can begin before the water reaches the subfloor.

Can wet carpet be saved?

It comes down to the category of water. Clean water from a supply line, caught quickly, usually allows the carpet to be saved by extraction and drying, while the pad is often replaced. Water from a sewer line or flood is contaminated, and in that case carpet and pad are removed.

Water sourceCarpet outcomePad outcome
Clean supply line, caught within a dayUsually saved after extraction and dryingOften replaced, sometimes dried
Clean water left for several daysMay still be saved, higher risk of odorUsually replaced
Gray water such as washing machine overflowCase by case, often removedRemoved
Sewage or flood waterRemovedRemoved

The category comes from the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard. Ask any company how it assigns category before work begins. For flood and sewage, our pages on flood damage cleanup and sewage backup cleanup describe the contaminated-water path.

How carpet is dried

  1. Extract water with a weighted wand so the pad releases what it holds.
  2. Lift or float the carpet so air can reach the pad and subfloor.
  3. Remove the pad if it is contaminated or cannot dry in place.
  4. Place air movers and a dehumidifier and monitor the subfloor.
  5. Clean and treat the carpet, then reinstall and restretch.

Floating a carpet means detaching it from the tack strip and lifting it. This lets air flow underneath. After drying, it is stretched back into place so wrinkles do not form. Some carpet backing can delaminate if it stays wet, which is a sign that replacement is the better option.

Smell and mold risk

EPA guidance says to dry wet materials within 24 to 48 hours to limit mold growth. Carpet that stays wet longer than that can develop odor and growth in the pad and on the subfloor. A musty smell after drying usually means moisture remains somewhere, and it is a reason to take moisture readings under the carpet.

When the carpet came out and the subfloor is exposed, readings show whether structural drying should continue. If the subfloor is wood and has been wet for days, the next step may involve hardwood floor water damage repair where wood flooring meets the carpet.

Carpet restoration cost factors

Carpet jobs vary with these details:

  • Water category, because it decides saving versus replacing.
  • Square footage and carpet type.
  • Pad condition and whether it can be reused.
  • Moisture in the subfloor or concrete below.
  • Drying time and equipment.

Insurers may cover carpet damaged by sudden water, while damage from long neglect often is not. Ask your insurer what it will pay for carpet and pad, and keep a sample of the damaged carpet if the claim requires it. Photograph the area before work starts.

Taking the pad seriously

The pad under carpet holds far more water than the carpet itself. Even when the surface looks fine, the pad may need to be lifted so the subfloor can dry, and the carpet can often be put back once everything beneath it is dry.

Frequently asked questions

Can I save wet carpet by drying it myself?

For a small spill, maybe. For carpet soaked through, the pad holds water that fans alone rarely remove, and the subfloor may stay wet.

Do you always replace the carpet pad?

Not always, but often. Pad holds contamination and is inexpensive compared with the cost of a recurring odor. Clean water caught quickly may allow it to be dried.

How soon does wet carpet start to smell?

It can begin within a day or two. EPA guidance recommends drying wet materials within 24 to 48 hours.

What if only part of the room got wet?

Water wicks. A patch that looks small can reach further under the carpet and into walls. Moisture readings show the true extent.

Is carpet from sewage or flood water ever kept?

Generally no. Contaminated water makes carpet and pad unsafe to clean in place.
