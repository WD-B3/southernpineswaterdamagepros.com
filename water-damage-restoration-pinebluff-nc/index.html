An older small town and what that means for water damage

In the early 1900s Pinebluff was a regional resort area, and it is now a small residential town. Lincoln Park School and the McLeod Family Rural Complex are on the National Register of Historic Places. Housing here ranges from older homes to newer ones, and older homes often have original wood floors, crawl spaces and older supply lines.

That mix points to specific problems. Older supply lines can fail, wood floors over crawl spaces take on moisture, and small leaks run unnoticed in rooms that are not used often. Our pages on crawl space water damage and burst pipe water damage explain those two cases.

When water is spreading in Pinebluff

Because Pinebluff is small and many homes sit on larger lots, water problems can go unseen for a while. If you hear running water, see a stain growing or find a soft floor, act at once. Shut off the main valve and keep away from electrical hazards. Then call the emergency line.

Shut off water

Close the main valve at the street meter box or inside the house.

Check power

Power to the wet area should come off at the panel, but only if that panel is dry.

Move belongings

Lift valuables, rugs and small furniture off wet floors.

Creek-side and low areas

Aberdeen Creek forms the southeast edge of Pinebluff, and heavy rain can raise creeks and fill low spots. After the Chantal flooding in 2025, culverts and roads across the county were overwhelmed. Check your address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center and ask the Town of Pinebluff about local drainage.

If water comes in from outside, see flood damage cleanup. If it comes in through a roof or window, storm water damage cleanup applies. Neighbors in Aberdeen can read our Aberdeen page, and homeowners in Pinehurst can visit the Pinehurst page.

After the water is out

Crews extract, map moisture and dry with air movers and dehumidifiers. In older homes with wood floors, drying is slower and monitored closely. Readings are logged each visit so you can see when the home reaches the dry standard.

What to tell us when you call from Pinebluff

Give your street, how long the water has been present and whether it came from inside or outside the home. We will explain the next steps and the scope and price before any work starts.

Small towns often have older plumbing and long driveways, so mention anything that affects access when you call, such as a gate, a long drive or a narrow road.
