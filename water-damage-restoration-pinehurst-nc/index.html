Around the Pinehurst Historic District

James Walker Tufts founded Pinehurst in 1895 as a health resort, and the Pinehurst Historic District is a National Register site and a National Historic Landmark District, with landscape design by Frederick Law Olmsted. Homes near the village center and the Pinehurst Resort golf courses range from older cottages to newer builds. The practical point is that older construction often has original wood flooring, plaster or thin subfloors, which dry and repair differently from new drywall.

If you own a home in or near the district, tell us its age when you call. It changes how we plan hardwood floor water damage repair and how carefully walls are opened. Ask the Village of Pinehurst whether any historic rules apply to exterior work before you rebuild.

When water is spreading in Pinehurst

Active water is an emergency in any house, and the first minutes matter most. If a supply line is leaking, close the main shutoff. If the leak is above a ceiling, keep out from under it. The cards below cover what to do while you wait for a crew.

Stop the source

Close the main water valve or the valve at the failed fixture. If you do not know where it is, find it now.

Power safety

Shut off breakers for wet rooms only when you can reach a dry panel safely. Never touch outlets in standing water.

Lift valuables

Move electronics, papers and small furniture off wet floors. Keep people and pets out of any water that could be contaminated.

Common water problems in Pinehurst homes

Many Pinehurst homes are surrounded by mature trees, golf course landscaping and irrigation, and that combination points to a few problems. Roof leaks from storm debris, irrigation or supply line leaks near foundations, and HVAC condensate issues in humid months are all familiar. See ceiling water damage repair for roof and attic leaks and AC leak water damage for condensate.

After a large storm, such as the heavy rain that hit Moore County in 2025, the order of work is usually extraction, moisture mapping and drying. Our page on water extraction explains that first step. The same crew can also assess whether storm water entered through the roof, which is covered on storm water damage cleanup.

How the work goes

We start with a short call. A crew arrives, stops or confirms the source, extracts standing water and maps moisture with meters and thermal imaging. Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers go in, and readings are logged until the structure reaches its dry standard under the ANSI/IICRC S500 approach. We do not promise an arrival time, but we do say when a crew is on the way.
