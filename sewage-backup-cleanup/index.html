Emergency Sewage Backup Cleanup in Southern Pines, NC

Sewage is contaminated water. Keep out, keep others out, and get it removed properly.

☎ Call (910) 387-8176 · 24/7

Emergency sewage backup cleanup in Southern Pines, NC is the removal and sanitizing of Category 3 water, which is water contaminated with waste. It comes up through floor drains, toilets and tubs when a sewer line is blocked or fails. Our emergency line is open 24/7, and sewage is one situation where waiting is not a good idea.

Keep everyone out of the affected area, especially children and pets. Do not run water, flush toilets or use the washer, because that adds to the backup. Do not try to clean it with household tools. Call a plumber to clear the line, and call us to remove and sanitize.

Sewage backup: what to do first

Contaminated water, electricity and gas

Sewage can carry bacteria and other contaminants. Avoid contact, wash well after any exposure, and ask a physician about health questions. If water reaches outlets or appliances, do not touch them, and call the utility or 911 before calling us.

  1. Stop using water in the house.
  2. Keep people and pets out.
  3. Do not touch the water or wet items without protection.
  4. Photograph the damage for your records.
  5. Call a plumber and call us.

Why sewage backs up

Backups usually start with a blockage from grease, flushed wipes or tree roots in the lateral line. They can also come from a collapsed pipe or a problem in the public main. The Town of Southern Pines Utilities Division says that sewer obstructions between the property line and the sewer main are the customer's responsibility, unless they are caused by roots in the right-of-way or pipe failure. It tells customers to call the Town so the cause can be determined.

That matters for cost and for who handles repair. If the blockage is in the public main, the Town responds. If it is in your lateral, a plumber does. Either way, the contaminated water inside your house still needs to be removed.

Category 3 water: why it is handled differently

The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard classifies water by contamination. Category 3, often called black water, is grossly contaminated and includes sewage. Materials that absorb it, such as carpet, pad and drywall near the floor, are normally removed instead of cleaned in place.

What cleanup involves

Crews wear protective gear, extract the water with pumps and extraction units, remove contaminated materials and bag them for disposal. Hard surfaces are cleaned and treated with an antimicrobial. Air movers and dehumidifiers then dry the structure, and readings are logged until it reaches its dry standard.

If the water also came from outside, see flood damage cleanup. For a first-floor or lower-level backup, our pages on basement water damage and water extraction explain related steps.

Sewage cleanup cost factors

Sewage jobs cost more than clean-water jobs for specific reasons:

  • Category 3 handling and disposal of contaminated materials.
  • Area affected and how far the water spread.
  • Materials removed, from carpet and pad to drywall.
  • Sanitizing and drying time.
  • Rebuild scope after the contaminated materials are gone.

Many homeowners policies exclude sewer backup unless a specific endorsement is added. Check your policy for sewer or drain backup coverage and ask your insurer. The plumber's invoice and photographs help document the loss.

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle sewage backup emergencies at night or on weekends in Southern Pines?

Yes. The line is staffed day and night. Keep everyone out of the area, stop using water, and call us and a plumber.

Is sewage water dangerous?

It can be, because it may contain bacteria and other contaminants. Avoid contact, and ask a physician about any health concerns.

Can I clean sewage up myself?

Small amounts on hard floors can be handled with protective gear, but larger backups and any that reached carpet or drywall should be handled by a crew.

Who is responsible for the sewer line?

In Southern Pines the Town's Utilities Division says obstructions between the property line and the main are the customer's responsibility, unless caused by right-of-way roots or pipe failure. Call the Town to determine which applies.

Does insurance cover sewage backup?

Often only with an added endorsement. Check your policy for sewer or drain backup coverage and ask your insurer.
