Water damage restoration for Southern Pines homes and businesses

Water damage restoration is the work of stopping water, removing it, drying the structure and cleaning what the water touched. When it is done quickly, floors, walls and belongings can often be saved. When it waits, water travels into subfloors and wall cavities, and a small leak becomes a large one.

We serve Southern Pines and nearby Moore County towns from a base on Pinecrest Plaza. Southern Pines sits in the Sandhills, where sandy soil drains fast but older homes still have aging supply lines, crawl spaces and roofs. The Town's own history goes back to its incorporation in 1887, so many neighborhoods mix historic construction with newer builds. Both kinds of home can flood, leak and flood again.

The causes we see most are plumbing failures, appliance leaks, air conditioner drains, roof leaks and heavy storm rain. The 2025 Chantal flooding showed how fast ordinary drainage can be overwhelmed here.

What a response involves

  • A call to the 24/7 line where you describe the damage.
  • A scope and price explained before work starts.
  • Water removal and protection of the affected area.
  • Drying with dehumidifiers and air movers, checked with moisture readings.
  • Cleaning, and removal of materials that cannot be saved.
  • Photos and notes you can share with your insurer.

Insurance

Insurance and water damage

Whether your policy pays depends on the cause of the loss, your deductible and your coverage. The NC Department of Insurance notes that homeowners policies generally do not cover flood or rising water, which needs a separate flood policy.

We do not name insurers or promise approval. We document the damage with photos and notes, and we explain what we are doing and why so your conversation with your insurer is clearer.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How quickly can you respond to water damage in Southern Pines?

The emergency line is answered 24 hours a day. We do not promise a set arrival time, because distance, traffic and weather change it, but we tell you what to do while help is on the way.

What should I do first when water is in my house?

Stop the source if it is safe, turn off power to wet areas if you can do so safely, move belongings out of the wet zone and take photos before cleanup.

Do you explain the price before starting work?

Yes. We explain the scope and the price before work starts, and you decide whether to go ahead.

Will my insurance cover water damage?

It depends on the cause and your policy. Flood and rising water usually need a separate flood policy, according to the NC Department of Insurance. We do not promise approval.

How long does drying take?

It depends on the materials and the amount of water. Drying is finished when moisture readings return to normal, not when surfaces feel dry.
