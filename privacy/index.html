Privacy Notice

What information we collect when you call, email or visit this website.

☎ Call (910) 387-8176 · 24/7

This notice explains what information Southern Pines Water Damage Pros receives and how it is used. We keep it simple because we collect very little.

Phone calls

When you call, we ask for your name, address and a description of the damage so we can help. Calls may be recorded for quality and training. If you do not want to be recorded, tell us at the start of the call.

Email

If you email us, we keep the message and use your contact details only to reply and to service your request.

This website

This site has no forms and does not ask visitors to create accounts. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and a map from Google Maps, and those services may receive your IP address and browser details when the page loads. Their own privacy policies apply.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share job details with your insurer at your request, and with others where the law requires it.

Questions

Email contact@southernpineswaterdamagepros.com or call (910) 387-8176. This notice may be updated, and the date of the latest version is shown in our sitemap.
