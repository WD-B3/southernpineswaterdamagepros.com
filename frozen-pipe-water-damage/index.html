Emergency Frozen Pipe Water Damage in Southern Pines, NC

A frozen pipe often does its damage when it thaws. Know what to do before the ice melts.

☎ Call (910) 387-8176 · 24/7

Emergency frozen pipe water damage in Southern Pines, NC usually shows up after a hard freeze, when ice splits a line and the thaw sends water into walls, crawl spaces or ceilings. Frozen pipe water damage is what happens when ice expands inside a pipe, cracks it, and releases water as it melts. Our emergency line is open 24/7, including cold nights and weekends.

If a pipe is frozen but not yet leaking, open the faucet it feeds, keep heat on and shut off the main valve if you cannot get to the frozen section. If it is already leaking, shut off the main valve at once and call. Do not use an open flame to thaw a pipe.

When a frozen pipe is an emergency

A frozen pipe becomes an emergency the moment it cracks, and the crack often shows up only when the ice thaws. Water coming through a ceiling, a wet crawl space after a freeze, or a sudden drop in water pressure after a cold night all point to a split line. Call right away instead of waiting for the thaw to finish.

Freezing nights do happen in the Sandhills, but a hard freeze here is occasional, and that is the risk. Pipes in garages, crawl spaces and exterior walls may have no freeze protection because nobody expects the cold.

Thawing safely

Never use a torch, heat gun or open flame on a frozen pipe. Warm air from a hair dryer, moving from the faucet toward the frozen section, is safer. If the pipe is inside a wall or you cannot reach it, call a plumber and keep the water shut off.

Where frozen pipes usually fail

  • Exterior walls with little insulation, especially behind kitchen sinks.
  • Crawl space supply lines that run along cold vents or open foundation openings.
  • Garage and utility room lines, particularly near overhead doors.
  • Outdoor spigots and the lines that feed them.

Ice does not always burst the pipe where it forms. Pressure builds between the blockage and a closed faucet, so the split can be several feet away from the frozen spot. That is why damage is found in unexpected places.

Cleanup after a frozen pipe splits

The steps are the same as any supply-line failure, with a few differences. Because the water often runs for hours before anyone sees it, more of the structure is wet. Crews extract standing water, open the walls or ceilings that took the water, map moisture and remove what cannot dry in place.

Then comes drying with air movers and LGR dehumidifiers, with readings logged each visit. If the leak ran inside a crawl space, see our page on crawl space water damage, because insulation and joists need separate attention. A plumber repairs the line, and restoration handles the water.

How to keep it from happening again

Insulate exposed lines, seal gaps where cold air enters, keep cabinet doors open under sinks on freezing nights, and let a faucet drip when a hard freeze is forecast. The National Weather Service forecast for Moore County is the right place to check temperatures before a cold snap.

Frozen pipe cleanup cost factors

A frozen pipe loss is priced by how far the water traveled:

  • How long the line leaked before it was found.
  • Whether the water stayed in one wall or reached ceilings below.
  • Insulation, drywall and flooring that must be removed.
  • Crawl space or attic access, which changes labor time.
  • Drying days and equipment count.

Sudden water from a burst pipe is often covered, but policies vary on freeze damage and on whether heat was maintained. Ask your insurer exactly what your policy requires, and document the damage with photographs before cleanup.

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle frozen pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Southern Pines?

Yes. There is no closing time on the emergency line, including the night a hard freeze hits. Shut off the main valve if a pipe has split and call us so extraction and drying can start.

How do I know if a pipe froze and split?

Common signs are low or no water at a faucet after a cold night, frost on an exposed line, and water appearing through a ceiling or wall during the thaw.

Can I thaw a frozen pipe myself?

Only if it is accessible and not yet leaking. Use warm air, never an open flame. If you are unsure where the freeze is, shut off the main valve and call a plumber.

Should I turn off the water if a pipe is frozen?

Yes, if you cannot reach the frozen section or if any leak appears. Closing the main valve limits the amount of water released when the ice melts.

Will insurance cover frozen pipe damage?

Often, if the damage was sudden and accidental, but conditions vary. Policies may require that heat was kept on. Ask your insurer before cleanup begins and keep your receipts.
