Storm season and heavy rain in Carthage

Water losses here track the weather. The 2025 Chantal flooding is the recent example. Carthage was part of the county affected, and town manager comments were reported in Carolina Public Press coverage.

Storms test roofs first. Wind-driven rain finds worn shingles, flashing and attic vents, and the water shows up later as ceiling stains. See storm water damage cleanup and ceiling water damage repair for how those losses are handled.

When water is spreading in Carthage

If water is moving, act on the source before anything else. Close the main valve, stay out from under a wet ceiling and keep away from outlets in standing water. Then call the emergency line, which is open around the clock.

Stop the source

Stay safe

Where the panel is dry and within reach, turn off the breakers feeding the wet room. Keep people out from under wet ceilings.

Protect contents

Move electronics and papers up and off the floor. Photograph the damage.

Water setting

The county seat and its older buildings

Carthage was founded and incorporated in 1796. The Moore County Courthouse and the Carthage Historic District are on the National Register of Historic Places, and the town hosts the spring Buggy Festival around the old courthouse square. Older buildings around the square and in the historic district often combine original construction with later renovations, which matters when a ceiling or wall is opened.

Many Carthage properties are small businesses and offices near the square. For those, our page on commercial water damage restoration explains downtime and documentation. For homes, tell us the age and construction when you call.

Ridge location and flood risk

A ridge location can lower the risk of rising water, but it does not remove the risk of roof leaks, plumbing failures or runoff into low spots. Check the FEMA Flood Map Service Center for your address and ask the Town of Carthage's Planning, Zoning and Permitting department about local rules. After a leak, structural drying keeps framing and subfloor from holding moisture.

Neighbors to the south can read our Pinehurst page, and homeowners east toward Sanford can visit the Sanford page.

What to tell us when you call from Carthage

Give the street or road, whether you are on town water or a private well, and whether the water is still running. Those details help us explain what to do before help arrives.

For older buildings around the square, tell us what you know about the roof, plumbing and any past repairs, since those details shape how we dry the structure.
