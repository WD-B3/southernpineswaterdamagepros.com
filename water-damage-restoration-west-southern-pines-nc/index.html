Water Damage Restoration in West Southern Pines, NC

Water removal and drying for homes in West Southern Pines.

☎ Call (910) 387-8176 · 24/7

West Southern Pines is one of the oldest and most important communities in the town. The Town of Southern Pines history page describes it as one of the first incorporated African American towns in North Carolina, chartered in 1923 and annexed into Southern Pines in 1931. Its center is the former Rosenwald school site at 1250 West New York Avenue, now home to the West Southern Pines Center for African American History, Cultural Arts and Business.

Where the neighborhood is and what is verified

Local sources place the community on the west side of town, near West New York Avenue and West Pennsylvania Avenue, where the E. S. Douglass Community Center is located. We do not have an official boundary map, so we describe the area by landmarks and do not claim exact streets. We also have no verified information on housing age, basements or crawl spaces in the neighborhood, so we make no claims about them. FEMA flood-zone status for this neighborhood was not verified here. Property-level lookups are available through FEMA's map service.

Storm context

When Tropical Storm Chantal hit on July 6, 2025, a Town release on flash flooding listed a sinkhole on West Pennsylvania Avenue at South Mechanic Street among its road impacts. We do not have a verified report of damage to homes in West Southern Pines, and we do not claim the neighborhood floods.

Water damage we help with

Most calls, in any neighborhood, come from everyday failures rather than storms. In older and newer homes alike, those include:

  • Burst or leaking supply lines, covered on our burst pipe page.
  • Water heater and appliance leaks that soak floors and cabinets.
  • Roof leaks that stain ceilings after heavy rain.
  • Air conditioner drain overflows during the summer.
  • Water coming in at doors or low spots during storms.

After water is removed, drying is what protects the structure. See structural drying and dehumidification for how dehumidifiers and air movers are used and how readings decide when equipment comes out.

Safe first steps

  1. Keep children and pets out of standing water, especially if outlets or cords may be wet.
  2. Close the main water valve if you can reach it safely.
  3. Do not use household fans or a regular vacuum on dirty or sewage water.
  4. Take photos of damage and keep receipts for anything you buy.
  5. Call us and tell us what is wet and where the water came from.

Insurance and records

Coverage depends on your policy and the cause of the loss. Photos and dated notes help describe the damage, but they do not guarantee reimbursement. Ask your insurer what it needs, and keep copies of every document.

Frequently asked questions

Where is West Southern Pines?

It lies on the west side of Southern Pines, around the former Rosenwald school at 1250 West New York Avenue. We do not claim exact boundaries.

How do you know when a house is dry?

Moisture readings in the wet materials are compared with readings from a dry part of the house, and equipment stays until they match.

We cannot say, because it depends on the policy and the cause. Call your insurer and keep receipts and photos.
